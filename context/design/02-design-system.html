Color Foundations

Tokens / Palette

Deep navy anchors institutional authority. Cerulean mid-tones evoke data in motion — freight routes, transfer lanes, live flows. Amber is reserved for AI interactions and high-attention moments only; its scarcity is what gives it power. Slate surfaces keep dashboard canvases readable without competing with data.

Signature Element

The Data Seam

The Data Seam is the system's single memorable device: a 2px cerulean rule (#2D8BC4) that appears at every section boundary, chart header, and stat card top. It evokes the physical manifest line that separates shipment records — a freight-native visual grammar no generic dashboard uses.

The seam is never decorative: it always marks a boundary between content types or levels of data hierarchy. This restraint is what makes it meaningful. The amber AI accent plays the same role in interaction design — reserved exclusively for AI-powered moments so users learn, through repetition, that amber = intelligence, and navy/cerulean = bank-grade certainty.
